1. The Operative (You)

Navigate with WASD or Arrow Keys. Your path is recorded as you move through facility corridors.

When the memory cycle expires, an Echo manifests to replay your exact previous route. In later sectors, up to 3 Echoes replay simultaneously!

◆

Collect all required Energy Orbs in the sector. The exit portal unlocks once all orbs are retrieved.
